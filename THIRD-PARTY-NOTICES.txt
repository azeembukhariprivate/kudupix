files, and the size of PNG, GIF, BMP and WebP pictures), reading video details (MP4,
MOV and 3GP files), the library, the photo fixes, finding duplicates, export and
printing, and People (grouping faces and naming them) is its own code.

It uses parts of Windows that come with it:

- Windows Imaging Component (WIC), to open and save pictures. Kinds of picture
  Windows has an extension for (such as HEIC with the HEIF Image Extensions from
  the Microsoft Store) open too.
- Windows Media Player / Media Foundation, through WPF's MediaElement, to play videos.
- File Explorer's own thumbnails, for videos and kinds of picture WPF can't open.
- The Recycle Bin, for anything KUDUPIX is asked to remove.

The tests (not part of the app) download sample photos (and OpenCV's sample face
pictures) from ianare/exif-samples
(https://github.com/ianare/exif-samples) and a sample video from ExifTool's tests
(https://github.com/exiftool/exiftool); they are not copied into this repository
or included in KUDUPIX.
